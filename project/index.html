Research Studio Lookbook 2026

A lookbook spread exploring structured typography, image systems, and editorial pacing to define a clear visual identity for Research Studio.

Dully 2026

A motion-led project for Dully, using pacing, rhythm, and contrast to build a sharper and more distinctive branded presence.

Much2026

Motion design system, defining timing, transitions, and movement rules to create a consistent visual rhythm across the entire identity.

Memory of a Flower2025

Webpage served as a digital postcard to send to your loved ones, for Alzheimer’s and Parkinson’s disease awareness. The flowers are drawn from memory of a woman living with Alzheimer’s.

Berries & Dairies2025

Berries & Dairies is an editorial style web experiment combining typography and motion, exploring how identity shifts dynamically across geographies.

Pas Normal Studios 2025

A refined take on the existing brand Pas Normal Studios, reworked for a capsule collection with focused layout and motion elements.

Tour de France 2025

A motion driven rebrand of the brutal mountain stages of the Tour de France, using dynamic type and rhythm to capture the intensity of the race and its iconic climbs.

Yocho 2025

Yocho is a playful identity experiment where bold modular shapes, oversized type, and simple interactions create a distinct front cover feel with strong character.

Champagne Towers 2025

Champagne Towers a surreal visual study combining reflective surfaces, stacked geometry, and light play to explore form, luxury, and celebration as graphic narrative.

Ekko Film Magazine 2024

Redesign of the renowned Ekko Film Magazine. Responsive editorial design built around using fluid grids and minimal type to create a magazine like reading experience in motion.

Udrust 2024

Udrust is a barebones utilitarian identity and landing page concept, where military inspired listing and morphing motion reinforce compatibility and storage management.

DJFEST 2024

An energetic microsite for DJ booking platform as well as casual music exploration, with animated textures, looping visuals, and bold type to mirror the intensity of a live music event.

Motel Industry 2023

Motel Industry is a conceptual minisite for a rave festival in Berlin. Focusing on atmosphere and attitude over conventional structure with the grittiness of sketchy environments.
