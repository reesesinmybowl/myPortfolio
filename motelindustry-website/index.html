This is a visual identity for an underground rave in Berlin called Motel Industry. Based on old TV monitors, signal interference and static. The project explores the aesthetics of the underground rave scene, focusing on the raw and unfiltered aspects of nightlife. It draws inspiration from the gritty, analog feel of early rave culture. References include late-night hotel rooms, pirate broadcasts and analog equipment. The aesthetic is dark, minimal and unpolished.
